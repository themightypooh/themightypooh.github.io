1. Overview

Geppetto Mobile is an offline-first 3D modeling, sculpting, CAD, rigging, and animation studio. No user account is required for core features. The app does not bundle advertising or analytics SDKs. Creative files stay on your device.

2. Information we do not collect

The developer does not collect personal identifiers, location, advertising IDs, biometrics, or payment data.

3. Bring-your-own AI keys

Optional AI Motion Assist sends prompts directly from your device to the provider you configure. API keys are stored encrypted on-device (Android Keystore-backed vault when available) and are never uploaded to the developer. Keys are not written as plaintext to SharedPreferences or logs.

4. EULA & commercial use

Geppetto Mobile is proprietary software. Subject to this EULA, you may install and use the application on devices you own or control.

You retain all rights to the 3D models, textures, sketches, rigs, animations, and other creative works you create with Geppetto Mobile.

You may use Geppetto Mobile to create assets for personal, educational, and commercial projects, including games, films, product design, and client work. No additional royalty is owed to the developer for shipping or selling those assets.

You may not redistribute, sublicense, reverse engineer (except where prohibited by law), or rebrand the application binary or its source. Third-party AI providers you connect with your own API key remain governed by those providers' terms.

The application is provided as-is without warranty. Offline-first modeling features do not require a network; optional AI assist features require your own provider account.
