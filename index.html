Released · open source

Geppetto

Editor tools for s&box: model, rig and animate without leaving the engine.

Effigy: a parametric modeller

  • Five workspaces: CAD with an editable feature tree, Model (edit, sculpt, retopo), Paint, Rig and Clothing. Exports a real .vmdl.
  • Full edit-mode toolkit: extrude, inset, bevel, loop cut, knife, bridge, live modifiers, UVs and weight painting.
  • Clothing is built from the body's bones, so it refits when the body changes. Also exports live cloth.
  • The .effigy format is plain text with one line per feature, so it diffs cleanly in git.
  • The modelling kernel has no engine dependency. It runs headlessly from C#, build scripts or a CLI, backed by a large test suite.

Marionette: an animation editor

  • Pose bones, keyframe with easing curves and auto-key, then bake clips or play them in game.
  • Two-bone IK, joint limits, and prop collision while posing.
  • Fits a custom model to the player skeleton and writes a walkable test scene in one click.

Midnight AM

A cosy first-person game where you host a late-night radio show over an 8-hour shift in real time: take phone-in callers, spin vinyl and decorate the studio.

  • An in-game retro computer with around 20 apps: a casino, chess, minesweeper, a terminal, a mixer and a vinyl press.
  • A built-in synth engine, song sequencer, voice recorder and WAV writer.
  • A fully interactive turntable: tonearm, pitch slider, lid and sleeves.
  • First-person arms with two-bone IK and pose blending, plus a save system with round-trip tests.

Survey Sentence

A prisoner, sealed alone in a windowless pod, finds and "prints" subjects using a sonar scope and a 3D printer. It's a PS1-style remake of my game jam entry Deep Survey, which you can play now.

  • The sonar scope turns an outside camera into echolocation through a depth post-process, displayed on a curved CRT.
  • Orders type onto the glass like a teleprinter, in baked bitmap fonts.
  • A true first-person body, with animation takes checked for how fast they swing the camera.
  • A layered sound director with ducking and loudness (LUFS) targets.

Inside The Arcade

A first-person retro arcade where every cabinet runs its own playable game, and many let you go inside them.

  • Around 20 cabinets: pinball (four tables), skee-ball, a physics claw machine, air hockey, a fighter, a rally game, a shmup, light-gun games and more.
  • Custom pinball physics with dot-matrix and video displays.
  • Games are tested headlessly with simulations and soak tests.
  • Per-station visibility gating and hashed screen redraws keep the busy floor at 60 fps.

Lab

Grip solver

Hand-grip authoring for Marionette. A custom asset stores blendable finger poses, and the solver closes the fingers onto the held object by testing against the hand's skin, not bone capsules. The core has no engine dependency and is unit-tested.

VT-07

A VR mech-cockpit pod at 1:1 scale. You climb in, the seat swings out to meet you, and you run the start-up ritual: initialize, five overhead toggles, ignition. It's now a cabinet in the arcade.

goonotes sync

A standard-library-only Python sync server. Per note, the newest edit wins. If both sides changed a note, the losing edit is kept as a conflict copy, and deletions travel as tombstones. An Android client is in progress.
